## Ink and shape
Use thick strokes with natural changes in pressure: some sections swell boldly, others taper thin. Most edges are solid. Restrict dry-brush roughness to one or two short segments; avoid a repeated scratchy filter. Allow occasional unfinished gaps and slight overshoots.
Use very few strokes, exaggerated character and object proportions, and plentiful white space. Solid black fills can sit slightly outside their outlines. Coarse halftone is the only shading.

## Color and composition
Pure opaque white background. Black ink only, except one small flat #EC1C7E accent where explicitly requested. No grey washes, gradients, shadows, ground, background plate, scenery, branding, or currency marks.
Objects should have a small oddness rather than generic icon proportions. Keep app drawings calm, witty and optimistic.
Text is normally excluded; the labelled team table is a user-requested exception. Functional glyph illustrations such as Ab, A, H1, @, percent, question marks, checks, and the language pair deliberately retain their defining marks. Ordinary props have no written labels, brands or currency symbols.

Use the suggested topics or type several subject words. Select Larger drawings, Icons, Edge characters, Guveno bird or References; combine this with a subject or purpose. Copy search link saves the current search, set, subject, purpose, sort and view; Copy drawing link includes the chosen drawing. These file links work on this computer; to share with someone else, send the ZIP and retain the index.html and assets directory together. The gallery does not upload or publish anything.
