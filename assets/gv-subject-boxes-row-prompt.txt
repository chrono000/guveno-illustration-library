Use case: illustration-story.
An original Guveno illustration: loose editorial brush ink on opaque pure white. Minimal witty designed forms and generous white space. Natural line pressure varies visibly, swelling bold then tapering thin. MOST contours are solid, with only ONE or TWO short dry-brush rough segments in the entire drawing; do NOT put a scratchy filter on every stroke. A few unfinished gaps and overshoots. Offset small solid black fills; coarse halftone dots are the ONLY shading. About 15 strokes per object, 25 per person. Calm, warm, quirky. No ground or backdrop. Black and white only unless this prompt specifically names one magenta accent.

Format 960 x 600 pixels; generous pure white margins on all sides, complete subject contained inside canvas unless entry from an edge is explicitly specified. Black ink only, no magenta.
